About Purr Chat

Purr Chat is a small, independently made website built around Mochi, a friendly cat companion you can chat with right in your browser. The idea started simple: a lot of chat experiences online feel cold or corporate, and we wanted something warm, playful, and genuinely fun to visit — a cozy little corner of the internet for cat lovers.

Mochi responds with a bank of cheerful, cat-flavored replies designed to feel upbeat and supportive, whether you're saying hello, having an ordinary day, or just want something sweet to talk to for a few minutes. It's meant to be lighthearted, not a replacement for real conversation or professional support.

This site is run by a solo creator who loves cats, cozy design, and building small web projects. Purr Chat is a hobby project, built and maintained independently, and it's still growing — new features and content get added over time.
