Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains how Purr Chat ("we," "our," or "the site") handles information when you visit and use this website.

Information we collect

Purr Chat's chat feature runs entirely in your browser. Messages you type are not sent to, stored on, or processed by any external server or third party — they exist only on your device during your visit and disappear when you close or refresh the page.

Like most websites, our hosting provider and any advertising partners may automatically collect standard technical information, such as your browser type, general location, device type, and pages visited, typically through cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad personalization settings at Google's Ads Settings page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on someone's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Children's privacy

Purr Chat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Doing so may affect how ads are personalized but will not affect your ability to chat with Mochi.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact page.
